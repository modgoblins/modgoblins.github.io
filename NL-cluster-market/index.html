New Life On ARK — Cluster Market

A player market, wallet and reward system for ARK: Survival Ascended, shared across every map of your cluster and set up entirely in game.

  • Player guideOpen the market, buy, sell, claim your items and use your wallet.
  • Admin guideFirst setup, roles, economy settings, rewards, logs and backups.

Support

Open a ticket in the Cluster Market area of the Mod Goblins Discord. In game, go to Admin → Diagnostics, press Create, then Upload, and add the link to your ticket.
