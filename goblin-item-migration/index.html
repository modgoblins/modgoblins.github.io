Goblin Item Migration

Move everything a tribe or a player owns from one map of your cluster to another. An admin starts the migration; the players collect their items, creatures and resources from a Goblin Item Migration Box.

  • Player guidePlace your box, find your items, take them or send them to your storages.
  • Admin guideScan a tribe, start a migration, give the box, manage boxes, settings and logs.

Support

Open a ticket in the Goblin Item Migration area of the Mod Goblins Discord. In game, open the admin panel, go to Diagnostics, press Create and add the link to your ticket.
